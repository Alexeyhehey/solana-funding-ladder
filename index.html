  7. 06

    On-chain raises

    Market-governed

    $3M raised in the Umbra case

    A live product and a community that already wants the token.

    • MetaDAO launches

      Amount
      Founder sets the minimum and cap. Umbra raised $3M (per its MetaDAO page).
      Terms
      Buyers have four days to commit USDC. Accepted funds enter a market-governed treasury. Founder spending and token unlocks follow the sale rules.
      Deadline
      Set by the launch
      Read launch requirements ↗: MetaDAO launches

¹ Standard grants, bounties and prizes are non-dilutive. A convertible grant may become an investment if agreed conditions are met.
